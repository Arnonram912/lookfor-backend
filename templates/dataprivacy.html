Data Privacy Policy

Your information matters to us.

Information We Collect

LookFor may collect student information, item descriptions, uploaded photos, and communication records necessary for lost-and-found operations.

Purpose of Collection

Information is collected solely to facilitate reporting, matching, verification, notifications, and item retrieval.

Data Security

Appropriate security measures are implemented to protect stored information from unauthorized access.

Data Access

Access to records is limited to authorized users and school administrators responsible for managing the platform.

Data Retention

Information is retained only as long as needed to support system operations and school requirements.

Your Rights

Users may request corrections or updates to information subject to institutional policies.
